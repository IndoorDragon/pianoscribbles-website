04 / MUSIC & MAKING

HarpForge.

A musical idea, made tangible.

I design and make sliding harmonica mouthpieces through HarpForge. The work brings together my experience as a musician with hands-on design, prototyping, and 3D printing.

Playing the harmonica myself gives me a way to explore the products in practice, alongside the process of building a small e-commerce business.

05 / IN THE WORKSHOP

the instrument.

PERSONAL INSTRUMENT PROJECT

Repairing my spinet piano

Working on my own spinet piano has given me an opportunity to engage with music from the mechanical side: looking closely at the instrument, working through a repair, and developing my practical understanding of how it works.

This personal project is part of a growing interest in instrument repair and the craft behind musical performance.
